👋 Welcome to Chase the Arena! Quick 3-Step Guide

How to Play (Super Easy!)

Your Space Rocket aims and shoots automatically! Clear Levels 1 to 10 and defeat the Level 10 Boss.

1
Pick Your Rocket & Level on the Home Page 🏠

Choose from 3 unique Space Rockets and progress from Easy (Level 1) to Extreme Boss (Level 10)!

2
Pick Power-Ups Whenever You Are Ready 🎁

When your Power Meter fills up, a glowing 🎁 Power-Up Ready button appears above the arena. Click it or press E whenever you want!

3
Use the Top Tabs for Shop, Boosts & Trophies 🏆

Switch between 🏠 Home, 🎮 Play Ground, 🛒 Shop, ✨ Boosts & Speed, and 🏆 Trophies anytime from the top bar.
